# Standard lane specs (research summary, 2026-11-22)

Naming (often mixed up):

| | Eternit | Beton |
|---|---|---|
| Official system | **Miniaturgolf** (NormMiniatur, DMV S 12, ÖMGV 2.1.2) | **Minigolf** (NormBeton, DMV S 11, ÖMGV 2.1.1), System Bongni |
| Abteilung | 2 | 1 |
| Lane | 6.25 × 0.90 m (±2 %) | 12.00 × 1.25 m (±2 %) |
| Target circle | Ø 1.40 m at lane end (centre ≈ 5.55 m) | Ø 2.50 m (centre ≈ 10.75 m) |
| Tee | 40 × 50 cm rectangle at lane start, 20 cm from each Bande | Ø 30 cm circle, centre 40 cm from lane start |
| Lanes | 30 standard, course uses any 18, any order | 18 fixed, order 1–18 |

All systems (IntSpR): hole Ø ≤ 120 mm; ball 37–43 mm; Grenzlinie red, Ablegelinien black. Ball may be repositioned 20 cm from a Bande, 30 cm from an obstacle (towards target).

**Not specified publicly:** Bande height/profile (only materials: flat iron, fibre cement, pipe), exact hole diameter, most Beton obstacle sizes (binding Bongni drawings held by WMF).
