# Miniaturgolf (Eternit, Abt. 2) reference notes, lanes 10-18, 20, 27

Collected 2026-11-23. Images live in `laneNN\`. Norm plan dimensions (NormMiniatur 01/2026) are deliberately NOT repeated here.

Conventions
- STATED = explicitly written in a source (quote + URL).
- ESTIMATE = my own reading of a photo, with the scale reference used. Treat as +-25 % unless noted. None of the estimates was measured with a tool; they are eyeballed ratios against the 90 cm lane width, the 1.40 m target circle, a ball or a person.
- Honest headline: **no source found states an obstacle height in cm** for any of these lanes (manufacturer pages, club pages, DMV beginner booklet, spare-part lists were checked). All heights below are photo estimates.
- Image quality: `flickr_*.jpg` are full-resolution originals (12-24 MP) from the Flickr account "coupleputts" (blog acoupleofputts.com). `funsports_*`, `harrislee_*`, `minigolfen_de_*` are only small web images (200-435 px), kept because they show a variant not found elsewhere. `dmv_anfaengerheft_pNN_*.png` are 220-dpi renders of scanned A5 pages of the DMV booklet "Miniaturgolf spielen - ein Ueberblick fuer Anfaenger" (several photos with players per lane, plus text). NN in the file name is the PDF page index; the printed page number is given below.
- Every image was opened and checked. Most were checked at contact-sheet size (320-660 px), about a dozen at full view.

STATED (S4 p.13): "Ein 'Labby' verfuegt meist ueber vier Eingaenge, jedoch ist nur der zweite Eingang von rechts zulaessig. Ausserdem liegt fast die ganze Bahn, ausgenommen das Abschlagsfeld, in der Schraege." / exception: "Wesseling. ESTIMATE
- Lane end height: target disc top is about 40-50 cm above ground while the tee end sits about 5-10 cm above ground, so a rise of about 35-45 cm over the roughly 5.3-5.6 m after the tee field (about 4-5 deg). Scale: leg length against the 90 cm lane width in the Solna side photo; player knee height in the booklet photo.
- The target disc continues the same incline in the Solna and Uppsala photos; a flat disc is the exception per the quote.
- Walls: vertical flat steel strip, about 8-10 cm high (2-2.5 ball diameters; scale: hole in the close-ups), a few mm thick, concentric arcs plus funnel-shaped entrance guides; thin bars run radially across the TOP of the walls as bracing and do not touch the playing surface. The outer ring is higher than the normal Bande.

- `minigolfbau_stumpfe_kegel.jpg` - S1 - BEST: both cones close-up, end faces visible.
- `rothe_stumpfer_kegel_part.jpg` - https://minigolfrothe.de/data/uploads/bilder/hindernisse/stumpfer_kegel6.JPG - single GFK part off the lane, seen from the large end (segment profile and mounting flange).
- `springe_stumpfe_kegel_top.jpg` - https://image.jimcdn.com/app/cms/image/transf/none/path/s2495adba1da9d704/image/id5b530e4d0b8b5ab/version/1537197650/image.jpg - near top-down view of the whole lane (2201x3654), yellow cones.
- `flickr_53133591978.jpg` - https://www.flickr.com/photos/coupleputts/53133591978/ (Uppsala, 2023) - green cones with player for scale.
- `kirchlengern_bahn12_stumpfe_kegel.jpg` - S7 - whole lane oblique, red cones.
- `dmv_anfaengerheft_p14_stumpfe_kegel.png` - S4 printed p.14 - three photos with players.

STATED (S4 p.14): "muss man zunaechst einmal zwei Kegel ueberwinden" / "Auf den meisten Anlagen wird die Bahn ueber beide Kegel gespielt" / "Die Kegel werden auch als Radkappen bezeichnet." / "Die Anordnung der beiden Kegel kann auf manchen Anlagen spiegelverkehrt sein." So the ball normally rolls OVER the low cone flanks. S3 lists the part as GFK.

3D form (from photos, consistent across 5 sources): each obstacle is LESS than half of a truncated cone lying on its side with its axis ACROSS the lane. The large end is a vertical flat face (a circular segment, flatter than a semicircle) flush against one Bande and standing well above it; the body tapers toward the opposite side and ends in a small low segment face; the long edges meet the plate along a small flange. One cone on each side (alternating); the surface is smooth and single-curved.

ESTIMATE: large-end segment height about 0.35-0.4 x its base chord (minigolfbau close-up, near cone), i.e. roughly 15-20 cm if the chord at the Bande is 45-55 cm (take the real chord from the norm trapezoid); small end about 3-5 cm high; the cone reaches about 2/3-3/4 across the 90 cm lane (Springe top view), leaving a flat strip at the opposite Bande.

STATED (S4 p.15): "es gibt unterschiedliche Ausfuehrungen der dritten Rampe. Manche sind aus Eternit oder Plastik und andere wiederum haben keine Rampe, sondern einen flachen Steg." / "oftmals ist dieser schmaler als ein normaler Auflauf" / "Es muss nicht zwingend Sand in der Schuessel sein, an manchen Bahnen sind auch Kieselsteine vorhanden." / "Der Wesselinger Sandkasten hat nur eine Rampe."

3D form: the wedges are plain inclined plates over the full lane width, rising from plate level and ending in a vertical drop, with triangular side cheeks; after each drop the lane is flat again. The bowl is a free-form GFK tub (wavy, scalloped raised rim; flower-like outline in plan) sitting in the target circle, with a narrow entrance notch facing the third ramp or bridge; filled with sand or gravel.

ESTIMATE: wedge end height about 20-25 cm (drop face about 0.22-0.28 x lane width in the Dreieichenhain and Neuwied views). Third ramp / bridge top about the same height; bowl rim about 25-35 cm above the plate, bowl interior about 60-70 cm across.

Also fuenf verschiedene Wege das Hindernis zu ueberwinden." / playing options: "Durch die Mitte (in den meisten Faellen) / Oder ueber eine der beiden hoeheren Wellen (eher selten) / Ueber die Aussenwellen links oder rechts (eher selten)".

The two channels beside it: the highest humps ("hoehere Wellen"). Outer two: in between. Entry and exit feather down to plate level; small mounting lugs at the corners.

ESTIMATE: highest humps about 15-18 cm (about 0.18 x lane width in the funsports front view and the Rothe part), centre hump about 6-8 cm, outer about 10-12 cm, ribs about 3-4 cm above the channel floor. Not stated anywhere.

- `dmv_anfaengerheft_p27_mittelhuegel.png` - S4 printed p.28 - two photos plus plan sketches of the round-hill type.

STATED (S4 p.28): "Der Mittelhuegel hat einen Huegel der in der Mitte der Bahn dort ein Loch hat." / "Die Materialien fuer den Mittelhuegel koennen aus Eternit oder Kunststoff sein." / "Die Hoehe des Mittelhuegels kann sehr unterschiedlich ausfallen." / "Sollte er am Loch vorbei in den dahinter liegenden Bahnenabschnitt laufen, waere es sinnvoll, dass er hinten abbandet" / "In Wesseling gibt es einen 'Mittelhuegel', der nur von vorne zu spielen ist." S3 lists the part as "Mittelkreis/Mittelhuegel (15)".

3D form
- Lane end: every photo shows a plain straight end with a Bande across (the ball is meant to rebound from it); NO target circle and no second hole.
- Wide type: in plan a circle clearly wider than the lane (it bulges past both Banden) with its own curved Bande; the surface is a shallow dome, hole on the crest at the circle centre; straight chords where the dome meets the flat lane.
- Straight-Bande type: hill only as wide as the lane. Only one small photo found; whether the surface is a dome or a barrel across the lane cannot be decided from it.

ESTIMATE: round hill diameter about 1.2-1.3 m (about 1.35-1.45 x lane width, Oaza photo); crest height about 8-12 cm at Oaza and Dreieichenhain, up to about 15 cm at Harrislee. Heights genuinely vary (see quote).

STATED (S4 p.17): "Der Vulkan, oder auch oft Turm genannt" / "Der Spieler sollte versuchen, die Auflaufbande moeglichst nicht zu beruehren" / "Diese Rundlaeufer, oben auf dem Plateau um das Loch herum, koennen ab und an mal passieren." / "Der Zielbereich ist das Zielloch und das komplette Plateau."

3D form (cone type, 4 sources): a complete truncated cone, not just a wedge, centred in the target circle. A chute with raised side walls ("Auflaufbande") is recessed into the cone flank and extends as a tongue out onto the straight lane; its profile is concave (gentle start, steeper near the top). The top is a small circular plateau with a raised rim, slightly dished, hole in the middle (the Rothe part shows a stepped recess around the hole). The other type is the ramp wedge alone.

ESTIMATE: cone base diameter about 1.1-1.2 m (leaves 10-15 cm of flat target circle all round; scale: 1.40 m circle in the minigolfbau photo); height of the plateau above the plate about 40-50 cm (about knee height of the Solna and booklet players); top plateau about 28-35 cm outer diameter including rim; chute about 12-15 cm wide at the top, widening to 35-45 cm at the foot; tongue starts about 0.5-0.7 m in front of the cone base. The older cones (Neuwied, Solna) look steeper and narrower at the base (about 0.8-0.9 m).

## Lane 27 - Raute
Images
- `minigolfbau_raute.jpg` - S1 - green rhombus frame on the lane, oblique close-up (best available).
- `funsports_raute.jpg` - S3 - blue frame, whole lane from the tee (small).
- `minigolfen_de_raute_thumb.jpg` - https://www.minigolfen.de/media/b4/6c/e7/1719519488/pic_hobby_miniatur_raute_k01905.jpg - 222 px thumbnail.

STATED: S3 lists the Raute (27) as a steel part. Nothing else found.

ESTIMATE: a hollow rhombus FRAME (not a solid block) of flat steel or square tube, about 4-5 cm high, sharp corners, lying flat on the plate in mid-lane with narrow gaps to both Banden. Poorly documented online: only one decent photo.
